# Tiny Planet: a convincing first reveal

Reusable design brief for composition, art and asset production. Implementation details live in
[planets.md](planets.md). Active work and acceptance stay in the existing
[My Tiny Planet project](https://linear.app/tabby-stack/project/my-tiny-planet-1d0a488cfd88).
Keep personal interviews, rehearsals and acceptance notes private.

## The experience

Interview → an inviting personal place → one memorable response → one optional personal
addition → a visible change in that same place. The first version already needs to be worth
exploring. Customization must not ask the visitor to repair basic composition or modeling.

Show, don't tell wherever possible. Objects, relationships, materials, light and action carry
meaning. Keep concise accessible labels, navigation, loading and recovery text. Treat concept
art, blockouts, engine captures and measured behavior as different kinds of evidence.

## Scene rules

| Concern | Rule | Review evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Meaning | Compose a recognizable activity rather than a monument to every interview word. | With labels hidden, the place and personal detail remain recognizable. |
| Arrival | One clear destination, readable personal hero and inviting action; frame quieter discoveries through a connected route. | Actual landscape and portrait first frames, at the size displayed in chat. |
| Relationships | Seats face usable surfaces; companions belong beside them; supports, entrances and walking space agree. | Approach, seated and reverse views; actual tap and walking checks. |
| Art direction | Share silhouette language, material finish, palette and motivated practical lighting. Decoration frames the subject. | Neutral asset views and in-engine scene lighting. |
| Behavior | A visible response precedes optional explanatory details. Motion follows the visitor's request and respects reduced motion. | A short interaction capture, including a usable way back. |
| Personalization | Suggest one appealing additive change immediately after reveal; preserve the existing world when chosen. | Before/after in the same view and a capture of the chosen edit. |

Light the activity, not the whole world equally. For a night gathering, contrast its warm
practical lights with cooler surroundings, and connect foreground, destination and secondary discoveries with terrain,
planting and a readable approach. Review atmosphere at the actual first-reveal viewport;
a wider camera that merely exposes empty ground does not improve composition.

Film framing must survive freely moving visitors. No single-view façade, forced introductory
flight or elaborate camera move should hide an unfinished place. Templates encode relationships
and anchors without forcing every person into the same theme or belongings.

## Generalization is a release criterion

Improve the reusable composition and asset system, then test a different person's world. Do not
promote a successful owner rehearsal into a universal default. A tropical scene's palms, pavilion,
shore, companions and night palette apply only when the interview supports them. Daylight, open
landscapes and other emotional tones need their own coherent treatment; more enclosure is not a
universal rule.

The current composer supports gathering and lookout scenes and a tropical setting. Shared
materials, water, paths and interaction craft are reusable; this small authored library does not
establish coverage of unrelated interests. Unsupported needs are coverage gaps, not permission
to replace the interview with a tropical dinner or astronomy scene.

Before claiming general one-shot quality, use distinct held-out interviews spanning activities,
settings, moods and companions. Freeze each input, render its first attempt on desktop and portrait
phone, then show its first meaningful action and one personal addition. Record asset/scene gaps,
input fidelity and visual feedback alongside failures. Fix the shared rule or accepted library,
then rerun all cases without manual per-person rescue. Keep a separate unseen set after tuning;
a regression suite that has been repeatedly polished is no longer unseen evidence.

Opening views must fit the actual composed bounds and viewport. Importance (focus, support,
environment) is separate from collision. Keep nearby personal additions in the framing intent,
and check focus visibility as well as bounding rectangles. Shared camera changes must also
preserve ordinary-planet framing and the loading-screen drag. Passing schema, geometry, terrain
seed and browser checks remain separate from aesthetic acceptance.

## First reveal and assets

First reveal uses accepted, available assets and deterministic assemblies. Bespoke AI generation
grows the library separately until measured experiments justify putting it in the waiting path.
A successful generation job, valid GLB or geometry check cannot certify visual quality. Required
hero assets must be ready before they are promised as finished. A fallback must meet the bar
on its own; a later swap cannot rescue an inadequate first impression.

Reuse generic material textures, palms, planting, terrain and rendering techniques already in
the engine. Preserve the storybook miniature style. Do not copy another person's architecture,
signs, collections or biography as a default.

Asset production: approved isolated references → consistent alternate views where needed →
pinned generator/settings → normalized dimensions/origin → surface/seat/attachment anchors →
optimized GLB → neutral and scene-light review → accepted library asset. Appearance, walking
footprint, visual envelope and interaction target are separate data. Static generated meshes
do not imply a rig, working seat or usable entrance.

Reference identity includes description, style content/version and generation settings. Geometry
identity also includes actual reference bytes. Preserve task IDs across network errors and
timeouts; preserve accepted files while replacements are pending. Cache migrations preserve
complete legacy assets rather than repurchasing the library. Existing generation caps and
spending approvals still apply; this brief authorizes no new spend.

## Proof and acceptance

Compare staging with assets fixed, then compare assets with camera/layout/light fixed. Include
actual action captures and portrait openings. Measure loading, downloads, decoded memory and
frame cost on target hardware; software-renderer checks do not establish phone performance.
Do not invent a universal aesthetic score, object count or “wow” threshold.

Use a fixed-input rehearsal with explicit owner feedback, followed by unseen interviews without
per-example rescue. A polished rehearsal does not prove general one-shot quality. Owner
satisfaction and permission to contact a tester are separate facts.
