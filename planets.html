2. Add it to your AI

Claude (the free plan works)

  1. On claude.ai or the desktop app, open Customize, then Connectors.
  2. Choose + Add, then Add custom connector.
  3. Name it My Tiny Planet, paste the address, and choose Continue.
  4. Pick No sign in, then Add.

The free plan allows one custom connector. Once it's added, it works in Claude's phone app too.

ChatGPT (Plus or Pro, on the web)

  1. Open Settings, then Apps & Connectors, then Advanced settings, and turn on Developer mode.
  2. Back in Apps & Connectors, choose Create.
  3. Name it My Tiny Planet, paste the address, set authentication to None, accept the warning, and choose Create.

3. Ask for a planet

Start a new chat, turn on My Tiny Planet from the + by the message box, and send this:

{{ prompt }}

It asks a few questions, then shows your planet in the chat. Walk over to the bar, sit down, read the chalkboard, toast a marshmallow at the campfire. Ask for any change and it shows you the new one.

Good to know

  • Nothing is saved anywhere. The link it gives you is the planet: keep it to come back, or send it to someone.
  • Anyone with the link can see it, so leave out your address, your phone number and anything private.
  • On a phone, the chat shows a plain card. Walk around it opens the planet in your browser.
  • Bits of my planet are still in there, like the hammock and the rocket. Yours takes them over as I build more.
